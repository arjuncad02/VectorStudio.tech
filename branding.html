Our Branding Process

Strategy First, Design Second

Great logos aren't just pretty — they're strategically built. Here's how we do it:

  • 1
    Brand Discovery Workshop
    We deep-dive into your mission, values, target audience, competitors, and aspirations to build a solid creative brief.
  • 2
    Moodboard & Direction
    3 distinct visual directions with inspiration boards — you pick the one that resonates most before we proceed.
  • 3
    Logo Concept Development
    We present 3 initial logo concepts — each with rationale explaining the design thinking behind it.
  • 4
    Refinement Rounds
    You give feedback, we refine. We iterate until you love it — revisions included in your plan.
  • 5
    Brand System Build-Out
    We expand the chosen logo into a full brand system — colors, fonts, patterns, templates, and brand guidelines.
  • 6
    Final Delivery
    All files handed over in every format (AI, EPS, SVG, PNG, PDF) organized in a clear folder structure.
