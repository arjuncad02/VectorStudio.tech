Our App Dev Process

From Idea to App Store

A proven process that takes your idea from concept to live app — without costly surprises.

  • 1
    Discovery & Scoping
    We define your MVP, user stories, and technical architecture. You receive a fixed-price proposal with a clear feature roadmap.
  • 2
    UI/UX Design
    Complete app design in Figma — every screen, every state, every micro-interaction — approved by you before development starts.
  • 3
    Agile Development
    2-week sprint cycles with regular demo calls. You see progress every two weeks and can reprioritize the roadmap as needed.
  • 4
    QA & Testing
    Manual testing on real devices, automated test suites, performance profiling, and crash monitoring before any release.
  • 5
    App Store Launch
    We handle App Store and Google Play submissions, ASO optimization, and respond to review feedback to get your app approved fast.
  • 6
    Post-Launch Support
    Bug fixes, OS update compatibility, analytics monitoring, and feature releases — ongoing as long as you need us.
