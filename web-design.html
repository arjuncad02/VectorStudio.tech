Our Web Design Process

How We Go From Brief to Live Site

  • 1
    Discovery & Research
    We audit your competitors, define your audience, and map out information architecture.
  • 2
    Wireframes & Moodboard
    Low-fidelity wireframes lock in structure; a moodboard aligns visual direction before pixel-perfect design begins.
  • 3
    High-Fidelity Design
    Full Figma designs for every page, reviewed and approved by you before a single line of code is written.
  • 4
    Development & QA
    We build the site, run cross-browser and device testing, performance audits, and accessibility checks.
  • 5
    Launch & Handover
    We deploy, set up analytics, and hand over training documentation so you can manage your site with confidence.
