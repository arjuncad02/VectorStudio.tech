FAQ

Questions clients actually ask

If yours isn't here, bring it to your free call — or email us anytime.

Who owns the website when it's done?
You do — fully. Design files, code, domain, and content are all yours. If you're on a monthly plan and later cancel, your site stays yours; you just take over hosting and updates.
What if I don't like the first design?
That's what revisions are for. Every project includes revision rounds (unlimited on Growth), and we share progress early and often so we're never far off course. You'll never see a "big reveal" of something you hate.
How long does a typical project take?
Most websites launch in 1–4 weeks depending on size and how quickly content and feedback come together. You'll get a concrete timeline in your fixed quote before we start.
Do you work with clients outside the GTA or Canada?
Yes — we're based in the Greater Toronto Area but work with clients anywhere. Calls are scheduled in EST, and everything else happens over email and shared project updates.
Monthly plan vs. one-time project — which should I pick?
Monthly plans suit businesses that want a site plus ongoing updates, content changes, and support without surprise bills. If you just need a site built once, ask for a one-time fixed quote on your call — we do both.
Can you fix or redesign my existing website instead of starting over?
Absolutely. Redesigns and rescues are some of our favourite projects — start with the free website audit above and we'll tell you honestly whether you need a refresh or a rebuild.
You're a new studio — why should I trust you?
Fair question. Everything in our Work section is real and verifiable — no invented logos or stock-photo case studies. You also work directly with the founder, get a fixed quote up front, and can cancel monthly plans anytime. We win trust by being easy to check up on.
